What an AI appointment setter actually does

A human appointment setter, BDR, or SDR spends most of their day on a short list of tasks: reply fast when a lead comes in, ask qualifying questions, answer objections, chase the leads who went quiet, and get the good ones onto the calendar. That work is repetitive, time-sensitive, and expensive to staff around the clock.

LeadSync automates that layer inside the CRM you already run on. It is not a standalone tool with its own inbox — it plugs into your existing GoHighLevel account and works your contacts, pipelines, and calendars directly.

Inbound: every new lead gets an instant reply

The moment a lead enters GoHighLevel — from a Facebook or Instagram lead form, a landing page, a funnel, or website chat — LeadSync responds within seconds. Speed-to-lead is the single biggest lever in follow-up: a reply in the first few minutes converts far better than one an hour later, and most businesses cannot staff that consistently.

The AI answers questions about your offer, qualifies the lead against criteria you set, handles the common objections, and books qualified prospects into an open calendar slot. You only get pulled in when a deal is ready.

Outbound: reactivate the database you already paid for

Most AI tools stop at inbound. LeadSync also runs outbound database reactivation. Tag a contact or a segment in GoHighLevel — old opt-ins, no-shows, dead pipeline, past customers — and LeadSync opens a natural, human-sounding conversation with them. It re-qualifies, handles objections, and books the ones who are ready now.

Those leads are already yours. Reactivation turns a list you have been paying to store into booked appointments. See how database reactivation works →

Channels: text and DM, not cold calls

LeadSync is not an outbound robocaller, and that is a deliberate choice. Conversations happen where people actually reply:

  • SMS — the core channel for fast follow-up and reactivation inside GHL
  • Email — longer nurture and follow-up sequences
  • Instagram & Facebook DMs — including real ElevenLabs voice notes, which land as personal rather than automated

Text and DM get answered when a call would be screened. A message meets the lead on their own time, keeps a written record in GoHighLevel, and avoids the intrusiveness and the extra compliance surface of unsolicited automated calling. If you want a phone-answering AI as well, run one alongside LeadSync — they do different jobs.

Does it sound human?

LeadSync is trained on your business, your tone, and your offers, and it writes short, natural messages — not a scripted chatbot with menu buttons. On Instagram and Facebook it can send genuine voice notes. The goal is that a lead reads the thread as a responsive team member, not a bot. Across 500,000+ conversations on the platform, about 35% of leads who reply go on to book.

You stay in control

The AI runs the conversation, but the handoff is yours to set:

  • Choose per agent whether LeadSync books directly into your calendar or sends a booking link with a clear call to action
  • Jump into any conversation in GoHighLevel at any time — the AI steps back
  • The AI caps its own follow-ups and stops chasing a lead who has gone quiet, rather than nagging them

Compliance and opt-outs

LeadSync honours GoHighLevel's Do-Not-Disturb and unsubscribe status, so contacts who have opted out are never messaged. If a lead replies STOP, unsubscribe, or asks not to be contacted, the AI stops immediately and flags the thread. You are responsible for having permission to contact the people in your database — LeadSync gives you the controls to run outreach cleanly, but it is not legal advice, and messaging rules vary by country and channel.

FAQ

Is LeadSync an AI SDR or an AI BDR?
Both terms fit. LeadSync does the appointment-setting work of an SDR or BDR — qualifying inbound leads and following up with your database — but as software inside GoHighLevel rather than a hire. It does not do net-new cold prospecting into lists you don't already own.
Does it work with my existing GoHighLevel account?
Yes. LeadSync is built as an AI layer on top of GoHighLevel. You connect your existing GHL account and your contacts, pipelines, and calendars sync automatically — it is not a separate CRM you migrate to.
How much does it cost compared to a BDR?
LeadSync plans are Starter $89/mo, Pro $289/mo, and Launch Sync Unlimited $449/mo (which includes done-for-you Concierge Setup). A single human BDR or appointment setter typically costs $3,000–$6,000+ per month in salary plus tools, and works one shift, not 24/7.
Do I have to build the AI agent myself?
No. The AI Co-Pilot builder walks you through it step by step, and on the Launch Sync Unlimited plan our team configures your first agent for you (Concierge Setup) — trained on your business, tested, and live.
Does LeadSync make outbound phone calls?
No. LeadSync is not a robocaller. It works over SMS, email, and Instagram / Facebook DMs, including ElevenLabs voice notes on IG and FB — the channels where leads actually reply.
Does the AI hand off to me, and does it know when to stop?
You choose per agent whether LeadSync books directly into your calendar or sends a booking link. You can take over any conversation in GoHighLevel at any time, and the AI caps its own follow-ups so it stops chasing a lead who has gone quiet.
How does LeadSync handle opt-outs and compliance?
It honours GoHighLevel's Do-Not-Disturb and unsubscribe status and stops immediately on a STOP or unsubscribe reply. You are responsible for having permission to contact your database; LeadSync provides the controls, not legal advice.
